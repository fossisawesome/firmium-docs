# Android TV

Firmium has a dedicated big-screen app for Android TV and Google TV devices, built for a remote
control instead of a touchscreen. This is an Android feature; the desktop app is not involved.

## Getting started

1. Install Firmium on your Android TV or Google TV device (sideload the APK, since Firmium isn't
   on the Play Store).
2. Open **Firmium** from your TV's app row.
3. Sign in with your server URL, username, and password using the on-screen keyboard.

## What you can do

- **Browse your library** — Home shows recently added albums, random picks, and artists as
  scrollable rows; dedicated Albums, Artists, and Playlists screens let you browse the full list.
- **Album, artist, and playlist detail pages** — open an album or playlist to see its full track
  list, or an artist to see their albums.
- **Search** — find albums and songs with the on-screen keyboard.
- **Now Playing** — a full-screen view with cover art, title/artist/album, transport controls
  (previous/play-pause/next), and side panels for the **queue**, **lyrics** (synced line
  highlighting), and **similar tracks**.
- **Smart Mix** — pick an energy level (and optionally a genre) to start a mood-based mix.
- **Equalizer** — turn it on, pick a saved profile, and adjust the 10 graphic bands with your
  remote's left/right buttons. Creating new profiles is graphic-mode only on TV.
- **Settings** — theme and font, crossfade (with duration and curve), gapless playback,
  ReplayGain, and account logout.

Navigate everything with your remote's directional pad and select button.

## Notes

- **Not yet on TV**: word-by-word karaoke lyrics animation, parametric EQ editing, importing
  `.toml` EQ profiles, Last.fm configuration, and cache/reset maintenance actions — use the phone
  or desktop app for those. They're either phone/desktop-only by design (text-heavy actions that
  don't suit a remote) or planned for a future update.
- **No persistent mini-player** — unlike the phone app, there's no playback bar while browsing.
  Open Now Playing to control playback; your remote's hardware play/pause button also works from
  anywhere.
- Playback settings you've configured (crossfade, gapless, ReplayGain, shuffle, repeat) carry over
  from your account, since TV plays through the same engine as the phone.
